Who We Are

Nature's Resources.
Purposeful Solutions.

Sri Sakthi Coirs is based in Pollachi, Tamil Nadu, a region known for its coconut-growing landscape and connection to the coir industry. We focus on coconut-based coir products for agricultural and industrial use.

Our product range includes cocopeat blocks, briquettes, grow bags, coco chips and coconut fibre bales. We aim to serve customers with dependable communication, careful product handling and attention to their quality requirements.

From product enquiries to packaging and shipping coordination, we work to make every business interaction clear and professional.
