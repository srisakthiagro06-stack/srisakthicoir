03 / LEGAL & CONTRACT PROTECTION

Every detail matters.

Contractual safeguards should be clearly documented before order acceptance.

Each purchase contract should define target moisture content, electrical conductivity (EC), acceptable tolerances, testing methods, compression ratios, sampling procedures and inspection timing. Rejection processes should be based on agreed evidence.

Contracts should identify responsibilities for freight, port handling, container detention, demurrage, customs clearance, documentation handovers and unexpected transit disruptions.

International contracts should specify governing law, jurisdiction, dispute escalation, arbitration institution, arbitration seat, language and applicable rules.

Commercial terms are indicative. Final payment conditions, quality parameters, shipping responsibilities and legal clauses must be confirmed in the signed sales agreement. This is not legal advice.
